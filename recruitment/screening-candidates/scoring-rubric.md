# Candidate Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to score each candidate consistently. Each dimension is scored 1–5.

## Dimensions

### 1. Technical fit (1–5)
How well the candidate's technical skills match the role's must-haves.

### 2. Experience level (1–5)
Years and depth of relevant experience for the seniority band.

### 3. Domain knowledge (1–5)
Familiarity with the product area, industry, or problem space.

### 4. Communication (1–5)
Clarity of written communication in the CV and any provided writing samples.

### 5. Trajectory (1–5)
Growth and progression signals across previous roles.

## Thresholds

- **Advance:** total ≥ 18, no dimension below 3
- **Hold:** total 14–17, or one dimension at 2
- **Reject:** total < 14, or any dimension at 1
